About Our Store

Serving Naupada & Thane West Residents with Care

Naupada Medical is a neighborhood medical establishment conveniently located at Gokhale Road, Naupada, Thane. We are committed to serving families across Shivaji Nagar and surrounding areas with reliable access to daily medicines, prescription fulfillment, and general healthcare products.
